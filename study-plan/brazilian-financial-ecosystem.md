# 🇧🇷 Brazilian Financial Ecosystem

> Essential context for software engineers working in Brazil's financial technology sector

## 🏛️ Regulatory Framework

### Central Bank of Brazil (Banco Central - BACEN)
**Role**: Primary financial regulator and monetary authority

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Monetary policy implementation
- Banking system supervision
- Payment systems regulation
- Foreign exchange control
- Financial stability oversight

**For Developers**: All payment systems, digital wallets, and financial APIs must comply with BACEN regulations.

**Website**: [bcb.gov.br](https://www.bcb.gov.br/)

### Securities and Exchange Commission (CVM)
**Role**: Capital markets regulator

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Securities market regulation
- Investment fund oversight
- Corporate governance standards
- Investor protection

**For Developers**: Trading platforms, investment apps, and robo-advisors fall under CVM jurisdiction.

**Website**: [cvm.gov.br](https://www.cvm.gov.br/)

### SUSEP (Private Insurance Superintendence)
**Role**: Insurance and pension regulator

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Insurance company supervision
- Pension fund regulation
- Actuarial standards
- Consumer protection

**For Developers**: InsurTech solutions must comply with SUSEP requirements.

**Website**: [susep.gov.br](https://www.susep.gov.br/)

## 📋 Legal & Compliance Requirements

### Open Banking (Sistema Financeiro Aberto)
**Implementation**: Phased rollout (2021-2022)
**Requirements**:
- API standardization (based on UK Open Banking)
- Data sharing consent management
- Security standards (FAPI, OAuth2)
- Customer data portability

**For Developers**:
- Must implement standardized APIs
- Strong authentication and authorization
- Data privacy compliance (LGPD)
- Regular security audits

### LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)
**Effective**: September 2020
**Key Requirements**:
- Explicit consent for data processing
- Data subject rights (access, deletion, portability)
- Privacy by design
- Data breach notification

**For Developers**:
- Implement consent management systems
- Data anonymization and pseudonymization
- Audit trails for data processing
- Privacy impact assessments

### PIX (Instant Payment System)
**Launch**: November 2020
**Requirements**:
- 24/7 instant payments
- QR code standardization
- API integration with BACEN
- Fraud prevention measures

**For Developers**:
- PIX API integration
- QR code generation/reading
- Real-time transaction processing
- Anti-fraud systems

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
**Key Laws**: Lei 9.613/98, Circular 3.978/2020
**Requirements**:
- Customer due diligence (KYC)
- Suspicious transaction reporting
- Transaction monitoring
- Record keeping (5+ years)

**For Developers**:
- KYC automation systems
- Transaction monitoring algorithms
- Reporting systems to COAF
- Document management systems
